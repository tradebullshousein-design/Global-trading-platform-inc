Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start trading?

Simply register an account, complete your KYC verification, deposit funds via fiat or crypto, and access our trading terminal to open your first position.

Yes. 95% of digital assets are stored in offline cold wallets. Fiat currencies are kept in segregated tier-1 bank accounts.
